FAQ

Questions? Let's make it clear.

What is digital marketing strategy? +

Digital marketing strategy is the structured plan behind your online marketing activities. It defines your audience, channels, messaging, content, customer journey, priorities and measurement approach.

What is brand strategy? +

Brand strategy defines how your business should be positioned and remembered. It covers areas such as positioning, audience, personality, value proposition, differentiation and messaging.

Can you create a strategy for an existing brand? +

Yes. Strategy can be developed for an existing brand as well as a new business. Existing brands can benefit from a strategic review, repositioning and digital growth roadmap.

Does the package include advertising spend? +

No. Advertising media spend, influencer fees, photography, videography and third-party production costs are separate unless specifically included in a custom proposal.

Can the strategy be customized? +

Absolutely. The listed packages are starting points. Scope can be adjusted according to your business stage, market, objectives, platforms and required strategic depth.
