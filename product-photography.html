Ask on WhatsApp ↗
Do I need to send my products to your studio?+

Yes, for studio shoots you drop off or courier the products to our Lahore studio and we return them after the shoot. For large items or locations we can shoot on-site.

Which platforms are the photos ready for?+

Every shoot is exported in the sizes and backgrounds required by Daraz, Amazon, Shopify, WooCommerce, Instagram and Facebook, plus full-resolution masters.

How long does delivery take?+

Essential packshots are usually delivered in 3–4 working days. Brand and campaign shoots are scheduled with you, typically 5–10 working days.

Can you also design the ads and posts?+

Yes. Our graphic design, social media and Meta Ads teams can turn the photos straight into posts, ads and print material.

Do I get the rights to use the photos?+

Yes. You receive full commercial usage rights for your business across web, social, marketplaces and print.
