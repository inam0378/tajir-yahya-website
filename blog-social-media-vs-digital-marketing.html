Social media marketing vs digital marketing: what's the difference?

By the Tajir Yahya Creative & Co. team · September 2026 · 3 min read

People often use these two terms as if they mean the same thing. They don't. Both have the same goal, business growth, but they use different tools, channels and strategies.

Social media marketing focuses on

  • Platforms like Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn.
  • Community and engagement with your followers.
  • Posts, reels and stories.
  • Brand awareness and staying top of mind.

Digital marketing covers much more

  • Paid ads on Meta, Google and other networks.
  • Email marketing.
  • Your website and landing pages.
  • Content and multiple digital channels working together.
  • Leads, sales and long-term growth.

In simple words

Social media marketing is one important part of digital marketing. A business that only posts on social media is using one channel. A digital marketing plan connects that channel to a website, ads, follow-ups and measurement, so attention turns into enquiries and sales.

Start where your customers are, then build the rest of the system around it: right strategy, creative content and a better digital presence.
